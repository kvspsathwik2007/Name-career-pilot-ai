# 🚀 CareerPilot AI

### AI Career Intelligence Platform

CareerPilot AI is an AI-powered career intelligence platform designed to help students and aspiring professionals understand, improve, and track their career readiness.

Instead of treating LinkedIn, resumes, GitHub, ATS analysis, career planning, and AI coaching as separate activities, CareerPilot brings them together into one intelligent career workflow.

---

## 🌌 Core Idea

CareerPilot follows a continuous career-improvement loop:

Analyze → Diagnose → Recommend → Act → Re-analyze → Improve

The platform evaluates multiple career signals and converts them into actionable recommendations.

---
